Install Markbeam Opens in its own window and works offline.

Safari installs from its own menu. Two taps:

  1. Tap Share, at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Markbeam then opens like any other app, with your documents already in it — they live in this browser either way, so nothing moves and nothing uploads.
